Each role added a different perspective on the same craft: steering the parties toward workable agreements. Coordinating at the highest level demands decisions made with the whole picture in view, under pressure. Governance demands reading the political and social context and bringing every stakeholder to the negotiating table. And human rights demands strict adherence to the law and to each party's guarantees.

That combination, together with a highly specialized professional team, is the foundation of the capabilities we now offer to projects and matters that need to move forward.
