## What data the site collects

None. This site has no forms, user accounts or comments. It does not use tracking cookies, analytics, advertising or third-party services that follow your browsing.

Like any website, the hosting server may keep temporary technical logs (such as IP address or browser type) for operation and security. These logs are not used to identify you or for commercial purposes.

## Information you share by phone

If you choose to call, the only information received is what you share during the call. It is used solely to respond to your request and, where applicable, to follow up on your matter. It is never sold, transferred or used for advertising.

## Your rights

At any time you may ask what information about you is kept, have it corrected, or request that it be deleted or no longer used, by calling the contact number.
