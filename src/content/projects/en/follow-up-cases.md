## Why follow-up matters

Many agreements aren't lost at the table but afterwards: when no one carries them forward, when counterparts change or when deadlines pass unchecked. Close follow-up is what gives the parties certainty and protects what was agreed.

## How we follow each matter

- **Comprehensive assessment.** Background, stakeholders, interests and the current status of the matter.
- **Tracking agreements and commitments.** A record of what was agreed, the next steps and who is responsible at each stage.
- **Legal follow-up.** Tracking deadlines, proceedings and requirements before the relevant authorities.
- **Ongoing engagement.** Constant contact with every party to resolve open items before they become problems.
- **Clear reporting.** You always know where your matter stands.

## Who it helps

- Infrastructure projects with commitments to communities or landowners.
- Organizations with open matters that require continuity and control.
- Companies that need an agreement turned into action.

Every matter is handled in strict confidence. That is why no individual cases or third-party information are published here.
