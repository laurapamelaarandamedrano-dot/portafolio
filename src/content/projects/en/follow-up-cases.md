## Why follow-up matters

Many agreements aren't lost at the table but afterwards: when no one carries them forward, when counterparts change or when time passes without news. Staying present after the agreement is what builds trust between the parties.

## How I support each case

- **See the full picture.** Background, the people involved, their interests and where things stand today.
- **Carry agreements forward.** Knowing what was discussed, what comes next and who takes part in each step.
- **Keep the lines open.** Close contact with every party to resolve questions before they become problems.
- **Communicate clearly.** You always know where your case stands.

## Who it helps

- Infrastructure projects with commitments to communities or landowners.
- Organizations with open situations that need continuity and good communication.
- Individuals who need an agreement turned into action.

Every case is handled in strict confidence. That is why no individual cases or third-party information are published here.
