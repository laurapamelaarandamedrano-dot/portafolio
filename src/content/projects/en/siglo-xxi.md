## The project

The Siglo XXI Highway (Autopista Siglo XXI) is the corridor connecting Pátzcuaro, Uruapan and Lázaro Cárdenas in Michoacán. According to public information, it is operated under a concession held by a company in which Promotora y Operadora de Infraestructura (PINFRA) takes part, alongside other partners.

## Why it matters

It is a strategic route linking central Michoacán with the coast and the port of Lázaro Cárdenas. People, goods and freight travel it every day, so any conflict at its toll plazas or along its sections has an immediate impact on the region and demands swift negotiation under pressure.

## In our experience

It is part of Salvador J. Vazquez Villa's track record on high-impact highway projects, where negotiation with local stakeholders, timely risk management and follow-through on agreements are as decisive as the construction itself.
